# Materials

Study instruments and participant-facing documents. These are the things a reader would need to evaluate or replicate the study design.

Typical contents:

- Survey instruments and item text
- Interview or focus group protocols
- Consent and assent forms
- Recruitment materials
- Coding schemes and rubrics
- Task stimuli

## What does not go here

**Completed instruments containing participant responses are data**, not materials — they belong in `data/raw/` and stay out of Git. A blank survey is a material; the same survey with 200 responses in it is data.

## Before this repository goes public

Check these files for anything that should not be public: pilot participant names in a protocol, an internal scoring key, a district contact's direct phone number, an unredacted consent form with a staff member's home address. Materials get less scrutiny than data and are a common source of accidental disclosure.
